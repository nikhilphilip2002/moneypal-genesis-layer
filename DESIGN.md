## Colors
This design system utilizes a high-contrast palette inspired by classic financial institutional colors updated for digital interfaces. 

- **Primary (#005DAA):** Derived from Pantone 300 U. Used for primary actions, brand moments, and active states. It represents confidence and stability.
- **Secondary (#000000):** Derived from Pantone Process Black U. Used for high-emphasis text and iconography to ensure maximum legibility.
- **Tertiary (#8A8A8A):** A neutral gray used for secondary text, borders, and decorative lines to provide structure without visual noise.
- **Neutral (#F5F7F9):** A soft, cool-tinted off-white used for backgrounds to reduce eye strain compared to pure white.


## Typography
To match the brand guidelines, the design system uses a pairing of two highly legible sans-serifs. **Harabara** is the primary brand font for headlines, capturing the geometric and approachable nature of Harabara. **Inter** serves as the secondary/body font, providing the clean, neutral, and professional utility of Myriad Pro with superior rendering on digital screens.
