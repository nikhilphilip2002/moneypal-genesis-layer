From  Data  to  Intelligence  
 
Why  are  we  building  this?  
Every  lender  today  has  data.  
Very  few  have  intelligence.  
Core  systems  such  as  Loan  Origination  Systems  (LOS),  Loan  Management  Systems  (LMS)  
and
 
Accounting
 
Systems
 
record
 
transactions
 
extremely
 
well.
 
They
 
answer
 
questions
 
like:
 
●  Who  borrowed?  ●  How  much  was  disbursed?  ●  Was  repayment  received?  ●  What  is  the  ledger  balance?  
They  do  not  answer  the  questions  management  asks  every  morning.  
●  Is  the  business  becoming  stronger?  ●  Are  our  investments  creating  value?  ●  What  would  our  regulator  worry  about  if  they  walked  in  today?  
The  Genesis  Intelligence  Layer  sits  above  operational  systems  and  continuously  answers  
these
 
questions.
 
This  sprint  is  the  first  step  towards  building  that  capability.

What  the  Developers  Should  Remember  
You  are  not  building  reports.  
You  are  building  answers.  
Every  chart  must  answer  one  management  question.  
Every  screen  must  reduce  decision-making  time.  
Every  number  should  lead  naturally  to  the  next  question.  
Think  less  like  database  developers.  
Think  more  like  executive  advisors.  
 
Technical  Architecture  
Prosper  (LOS/LMS)                  \                   \                    --->  Genesis  Intelligence  Layer                   /                  /          Tally  ERP  (GL)                       ↓           Business  Intelligence           Finance  Intelligence           Regulatory  Intelligence   
Genesis  does  not  modify  source  systems.  
It  continuously  interprets  them.
