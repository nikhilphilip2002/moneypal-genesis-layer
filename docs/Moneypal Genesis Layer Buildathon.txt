6.  AI  Expectations  
The  solution  shall  use  the  Aroha  Corporate  Intelligence  RAG  Framework  to:  
●  Ingest  documents  ●  Index  content  ●  Perform  semantic  search  ●  Generate  executive  summaries  ●  Compare  competitors  ●  Produce  AI  briefings  ●  Link  every  summary  back  to  its  source  
The  application  should  demonstrate  explainable  AI  rather  than  chatbot-style  responses.  
 
7.  Technology  
Teams  may  choose  their  preferred  technology  stack.  
The  focus  is  on:  
●  Modular  architecture  ●  Clean  APIs  ●  Responsive  UI  ●  Extensible  design  ●  Maintainable  code  
Authentication  may  be  implemented  using  demonstration  credentials.

8.  Evaluation  
Selection  of  engineers  will  be  based  on:  
Area  Weight  
Software  Architecture  30%  
Product  Thinking  20%  
User  Experience  20%  
AI  Integration  15%  
Engineering  Quality  15%  
We  are  looking  for  engineers  who  can  build  production  platforms—not  simply  hackathon  
prototypes.
 
 
9.  What  Happens  Next  
Following  the  Buildathon,  the  selected  team  will  extend  the  Genesis  Layer  to  include:  
●  Prosper  migration  ●  Tally  migration  ●  Canonical  Business  Objects  ●  NEST  Aggregate  Block  creation  ●  Business  Intelligence  ●  Regulatory  Information  Model  (RIM)  ●  DNBS  reporting  ●  Institutional  onboarding  workflows  
The  Buildathon  Console  will  therefore  evolve  directly  into  the  production  Genesis  Platform.
